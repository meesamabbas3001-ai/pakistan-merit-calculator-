How to Check KP Board Results

  • Online: board's official website → HSSC Results → enter your roll number for the detailed marks sheet (DMC).
  • By SMS: for Bannu and Swat, send your roll number to 9818 — the quickest option when board websites are overloaded.
  • Gazette: institutions can download the result gazette from the board website.

New Grading System in KP Boards

KP boards including Bannu and Swat now follow the IBCC grading framework: the passing mark has risen to 40%, certificates use a 10-point grade scale from A++ to U, and the word "Fail" has been replaced with "Unsatisfactory" (U) on the DMC. Students who disagree with their total can apply online for re-totalling, normally within 15 days of the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have the KP board HSSC results 2026 been announced?

BISE Bannu and BISE Swat officially announced their HSSC Part-I and Part-II results in September 2026. Other KP boards announce on their own dates — check your board's official website.

How can I check my Bannu or Swat board result by SMS?

Send your roll number to 9818 and your marks are texted back to you. You can also check online at biseb.edu.pk (Bannu) or bisess.edu.pk (Swat).

What are the passing marks in KP boards in 2026?

KP boards now follow the IBCC framework with a 40% passing mark and a 10-point A++ to U grading scale; 'Fail' is shown as 'Unsatisfactory' (U) on the DMC.

How do I apply for re-totalling in a KP board?

Through your board's online portal, normally within 15 days of the result announcement.
