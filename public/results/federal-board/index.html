Federal Board Result 2026 — FBISE Islamabad

The Federal Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (FBISE), Islamabad, has announced the HSSC results for 2026. Both 11th and 12th class results came out together.

How to Check Federal Board Result 2026

  • Online by roll number: fbise.edu.pk → Results → select HSSC-I or HSSC-II Annual 2026 → enter roll number (or name) → submit.
  • By SMS: send FB [space] roll number to 5050 and your subject-wise result is texted back.
  • Gazette: the combined result gazette is available through the FBISE result system for institutions and candidates.

Frequently Asked Questions

When was the FBISE HSSC result 2026 announced?

FBISE announced both HSSC Part-I (11th class) and Part-II (12th class) results on Wednesday, 9 September 2026 at 11:30 AM.

How do I check my Federal Board result by SMS?

Type FB, add a space and your roll number (for example: FB 123456) and send the message to 5050.

What is the official website of the Federal Board?

fbise.edu.pk — results, gazettes and rechecking information are published there.

Can I check my FBISE result by name?

Yes, the FBISE result portal allows searching by roll number and by name for the announced examinations.
