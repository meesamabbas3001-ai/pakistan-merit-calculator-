How to Check Punjab Board Result Online

  • Visit your board's official website and open the Results section.
  • Select HSSC/SSC Annual 2026 and your class (Part-I or Part-II).
  • Enter your roll number and click Search — your result card appears on screen.
  • Most boards also offer search by name and the downloadable gazette on result day.

Passing marks in Punjab boards are 33% in each subject. If you are not satisfied with a result, rechecking applications open through the board's website within about 15 days of the announcement.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will the 11th class result 2026 Punjab Board be announced?

On 22 October 2026 at 10:00 AM, for all nine Punjab boards at the same time. Position holders are announced on 21 October 2026.

What is the Lahore Board SMS code for result checking?

Send your roll number to 800291 for BISE Lahore. Other codes: Gujranwala 800299, Multan 800293, Faisalabad 800240, Rawalpindi 800296, Sargodha 800290, DG Khan 800295, Bahawalpur 800298, Sahiwal 800292.

When was the Punjab 12th class result 2026 announced?

The 2nd year (HSSC Part-II) result was announced on 23 September 2026, and result cards have been issued to institutions and private candidates.

Can I check my Punjab board result by name?

Yes. Most Punjab boards provide a Search by Name option on their official result page on the day of the announcement, and the gazette lists every candidate.

What are the passing marks in Punjab boards?

33 percent in each subject for Punjab boards in 2026.
