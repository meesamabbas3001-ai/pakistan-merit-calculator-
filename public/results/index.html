How to Check Your Board Result

1. Online by roll number (official board website)

Open your board's official website, go to the Results section, select your class and exam (HSSC/SSC Annual 2026), enter your roll number and submit. Your detailed marks sheet appears on screen. This is the only official online method — beware of fake result websites asking for personal data.

2. By SMS (works without internet)

Send your roll number to your board's SMS code and the result is texted back to you. Punjab boards each have their own code (for example Lahore 800291, Multan 800293) — the full list is on the Punjab boards page. Federal Board: type FB, a space and your roll number, and send to 5050. KP boards (Bannu, Swat): send roll number to 9818. Karachi (BIEK): send roll number to 8583.

3. Result gazette

Every board publishes a result gazette (PDF/CD) listing all candidates by roll number, usually a few hours after the online result. Schools and academies use the gazette; it is also the fallback when you have lost your roll number slip, because many boards also allow a search by name.

Other Boards

The remaining Sindh boards (Hyderabad, Sukkur, Larkana, Mirpurkhas, Shaheed Benazirabad), the Balochistan Board (Quetta) and the AJK Board (Mirpur) announce results on their own schedules on their official websites. Check the board's website on the day your class's result is expected, and use the SMS or gazette method if the website is slow under load.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will the 11th class result 2026 be announced in Punjab?

All nine Punjab boards will announce the 1st year (HSSC Part-I) result together on 22 October 2026 at 10:00 AM. Position holders are announced on 21 October 2026.

How can I check my board result by roll number?

Go to your board's official website, open the Results section, select your class and the Annual 2026 exam, enter your roll number and submit. You can also check by SMS or through the result gazette.

What is the SMS code to check Punjab board results?

Each Punjab board has its own code: Lahore 800291, Gujranwala 800299, Multan 800293, Faisalabad 800240, Rawalpindi 800296, Sargodha 800290, DG Khan 800295, Bahawalpur 800298, Sahiwal 800292. Send your roll number to your board's code.

When was the Federal Board HSSC result 2026 announced?

FBISE announced the HSSC Part-I and Part-II (11th and 12th class) results on 9 September 2026 at 11:30 AM. Students can check by roll number on fbise.edu.pk or by sending FB followed by their roll number to 5050.
