How to Check 1st Year Result 2026

  • By roll number (online): board's official website → Results → HSSC Part-I Annual 2026 → enter roll number.
  • By SMS: send your roll number to your board's code (table above; full Punjab list on the Punjab boards page). Fastest method when websites crash on result day.
  • By name: most Punjab boards offer a "Search by Name" option on the result page on announcement day.
  • Gazette: the full result gazette is published a few hours after the online result.

Why the 1st Year Result Matters for University Admission

Your HSSC Part-I marks already count. Many universities calculate a provisional merit using Part-I marks before the final Intermediate result — read our guide on Inter Part-1 provisional merit. And in the final aggregate formulas, Intermediate carries serious weight (for example 40% in the MDCAT formula and 50% in UET's ECAT formula), so a strong 1st year result is the foundation of your final merit. After your result, try the free aggregate calculator with your Part-I marks to see where you stand.

If You Get a Supply (Supplementary)

Students who fall short in one or two subjects can appear in the supplementary examinations, which Punjab boards are expected to hold in November 2026. If more than two subjects are not cleared, the year usually has to be repeated — confirm the exact rules and dates on your board's website.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 1st year result 2026 date for Punjab boards?

22 October 2026 at 10:00 AM for all nine Punjab boards together. Position holders are announced on 21 October 2026.

How do I check my 11th class result by SMS?

Send your roll number to your board's SMS code. Punjab codes include Lahore 800291 and Multan 800293; Federal Board uses FB + roll number to 5050; Karachi uses 8583; KP boards (Bannu, Swat) use 9818.

Has the Karachi Board 1st year result 2026 been announced?

Yes, in stages: HSC Part-I Pre-Engineering was announced on 2 October 2026 and Pre-Medical in October 2026. Remaining groups are being announced during October on biek.edu.pk.

Does the 1st year result affect university merit?

Yes. Universities use Part-I marks for provisional merit lists, and final aggregate formulas give Intermediate major weight — 40% in MDCAT and 50% in the UET ECAT formula.

What if I fail one subject in 1st year?

You can usually appear in the supplementary exams, expected in November 2026 for Punjab boards. Check your board's official website for the exact schedule and rules.
