How to Check Karachi Board Result

  • Online: go to biek.edu.pk → Results → select your group and Part-I/Part-II → enter your roll number.
  • By SMS: type your roll number and send it to 8583.
  • BIEK app: results are also published on the board's official Android application (search "BIEK" on the Play Store).
  • Gazette: BIEK issues the official result gazette for every group; colleges receive marks certificates within about two weeks.
Karachi's matric board (BSEK) is a separate board from BIEK. If you are a 9th or 10th class student, your result is announced by BSEK Karachi on its own schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

Has the BIEK HSC Part 1 result 2026 been announced?

Yes, group by group: Pre-Engineering on 2 October 2026 and Pre-Medical in October 2026. Remaining Part-I groups are announced in stages during October 2026 on biek.edu.pk.

How can I check my Karachi Board result by SMS?

Type your roll number in an SMS and send it to 8583. You can also check online at biek.edu.pk or through the official BIEK Android app.

When was the BIEK Commerce result 2026 announced?

The HSC Part-II Commerce Regular and Commerce Private results were announced on 14 September 2026.

What is the official website of Karachi Board (BIEK)?

biek.edu.pk — the Board of Intermediate Education Karachi publishes all HSC results and gazettes there.
